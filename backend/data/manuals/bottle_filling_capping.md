# Liquid Bottle Filling & Capping Machine FC-8 — Service Manual
Machine type: Filler-Capper
Manufacturer: Packtech Systems (synthetic manual for demo use)

## 1. Overview
8-head volumetric piston filler with ROPP / screw capper for oral liquids (syrups). Fill volume 30-200 ml, accuracy ±1%. Capping torque 1.2-1.8 Nm. Speed 60-80 bottles/min.

## 2. Alarm / Error Codes
- F02 FILL VOLUME OUT OF TOLERANCE: piston seal wear, air in product line, wrong dose setting.
- F05 NO BOTTLE NO FILL: sensor dirty.
- F07 CAPPING TORQUE HIGH/LOW: clutch wear, cap chute jam.
- F10 LOW AIR PRESSURE.

## 3. Troubleshooting — Underfill / Overfill
1. Check weight of 10 consecutive bottles per head on calibrated balance; deviation should be within ±1.5%.
2. Replace piston O-rings and non-return valves (part PS-KIT-FC8) if one head drifts.
3. Bleed air from product hopper and hoses; maintain product level above 30%.
4. Product viscosity changes with temperature — keep product at 25 ± 2 °C.
5. Drip from nozzles: check anti-drip valve and suck-back setting.

## 4. Troubleshooting — Capping Problems / Loose Caps
Adjust magnetic clutch torque; replace capping chuck inserts when worn. Cross-threading caused by misaligned bottle under chuck — check gripper star wheel.

## 5. Troubleshooting — Pneumatic Pressure Low
Same procedure as general pneumatic leak check: FRL filter, fittings, cylinder seals, solenoid valves.

## 6. Preventive Maintenance
- Daily: CIP/clean nozzles, fill-weight check every hour (IPQC).
- Weekly: inspect piston seals, lubricate cam track.
- Monthly: replace O-ring kit as needed, calibrate torque tester.
