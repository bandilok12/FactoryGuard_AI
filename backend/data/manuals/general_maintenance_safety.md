# General Maintenance, Lubrication & Safety SOP
Machine type: All
Document: SOP-ENG-001 (synthetic, for demo use)

## 1. Lock-Out / Tag-Out (LOTO)
1. Inform the shift operator and supervisor. 2. Stop machine using normal stop. 3. Isolate main electrical isolator and apply personal lock and tag. 4. Isolate and bleed compressed air. 5. Allow heaters to cool below 50 °C. 6. Verify zero energy by trying to start. 7. Record LOTO in the maintenance ticket.

## 2. Vibration Severity (ISO 10816-3, small machines)
- Zone A: below 2.8 mm/s RMS — new machine condition.
- Zone B: 2.8-4.5 mm/s — acceptable for long-term operation.
- Zone C: 4.5-7.1 mm/s — plan maintenance soon; increase monitoring.
- Zone D: above 7.1 mm/s — damage likely; stop and repair.
A rising trend is more important than a single reading.

## 3. Bearing Replacement Procedure
1. LOTO. 2. Mark shaft position. 3. Remove bearing with puller (never hammer the outer race). 4. Clean shaft, check for scoring. 5. Heat new bearing with induction heater to 80-100 °C. 6. Slide on to shoulder. 7. Pack with NLGI-2 grease to 30-50% of free volume. 8. Re-assemble, check alignment, run 15 minutes, measure vibration and temperature.

## 4. Pneumatic System Leak Check
Pressure at machine must be 6 ± 0.5 bar. Drain FRL water daily. A pressure drop greater than 0.5 bar across FRL filter means the element is choked. Check fittings with soap solution. Typical leak sources: push-in fittings, cylinder rod seals, solenoid valve exhaust.

## 5. Thermocouple & Heater Checks
K-type thermocouple: measure mV against reference table, or compare with calibrated probe (tolerance ±2 °C). Heater cartridge resistance R = V²/P (e.g., 230 V, 1.3 kW ≈ 40 Ω). Open circuit = failed heater.

## 6. Motor Overload / Overheating
Check supply voltage balance (< 2%), motor current against nameplate, cooling fan, and mechanical load. Insulation resistance > 1 MΩ at 500 V DC.

## 7. Lubrication Chart
- Ball bearings: NLGI-2 lithium grease, every 500 h.
- Gearboxes: ISO VG 220 gear oil, change every 2,500 h.
- Chains: food-grade H1 oil, weekly.
- Guide rails: H1 grease, weekly.

## 8. Root Cause Analysis
Use 5-Why and fishbone (Man, Machine, Method, Material, Measurement, Environment) for any breakdown longer than 60 minutes or any repeat failure within 30 days. Record root cause and corrective/preventive action (CAPA) in the ticket.

## 9. GMP Line Clearance After Maintenance
After any maintenance in the product-contact area: remove tools and spare parts, clean and sanitise with 70% IPA, get QA line clearance before restarting production. Record in the batch manufacturing record.
